Where the icons live

Today the four secondary controls — star, karaoke, AirPlay, share — sit in three different orders across the player's three views. Two of them are conditional (AirPlay only in Safari with a target; karaoke only when the song has synced lyrics), so the real test of any scheme is how the row reads when they drop out. Use the toggles to see each scheme with icons present and absent.
